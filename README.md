# Astersky

**A more open, independent, and welcoming future for social networking.**

Astersky is an independent initiative focused on making participation in the [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com/) ecosystem more accessible, portable, and community-oriented.

We believe social networking should be built around open standards, user choice, and the freedom to participate without being tied to a single platform or service provider.

Our goal is to help make that vision practical by developing and supporting independent services and tools that strengthen the broader AT Protocol ecosystem.

## What we're working towards

Astersky aims to build an ecosystem of services that make participating in AT Protocol easier and more accessible.

Our areas of interest include:

- **Identity and account services** » Making it easier for people to establish, manage, and maintain their identities across the ecosystem.
- **Community infrastructure** » Supporting welcoming communities through independent services and community-oriented tools.
- **Moderation and safety** » Exploring better ways to support community moderation, user choice, and safer social experiences.
- **Social applications** » Developing applications that make participating in the open social web approachable and enjoyable.
- **Independent infrastructure** » Supporting a more diverse ecosystem of service providers and reducing unnecessary reliance on individual platforms.

These areas will evolve as the project develops.

## Our principles

Astersky is guided by a few core principles:

- **Openness:** Build on open protocols and interoperable standards rather than proprietary ecosystems.
- **Independence:** Encourage a diverse ecosystem where no single provider needs to control the entire experience.
- **Portability:** Respect people's identities, data, and ability to move between service providers.
- **Community:** Create welcoming spaces that respect different backgrounds, identities, and experiences.
- **Privacy:** Minimize unnecessary data collection and respect user autonomy.
- **Accessibility:** Make participation approachable regardless of technical experience or ability.

## Built on AT Protocol

Astersky is built around the [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com/), an open framework for decentralized social networking.

We see AT Protocol as an opportunity to build services that work together while allowing individuals and communities greater control over their social experiences.

Astersky is an independent project within this ecosystem. We are not affiliated with Bluesky, nor are we intended to be a replacement for it.

## Development

Astersky is an evolving project. We intend to build incrementally, prioritize practical solutions, and make use of existing open-source technologies wherever they make sense.

Not everything needs to be reinvented, and not every part of the ecosystem needs another implementation. We want to focus on the areas where independent development can provide meaningful value.

Our repositories will evolve alongside the project, with individual projects documenting their own technical details and development goals.
